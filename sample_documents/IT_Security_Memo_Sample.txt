MEMORANDUM

TO:      All Staff, Acme Corporation (Fictitious Company)
FROM:    IT Security Department
DATE:    January 15, 2026
RE:      Mandatory Password Policy Update and Phishing Awareness

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This memo outlines updated IT security requirements effective
February 1, 2026. All employees, contractors, and temporary staff
with access to company systems must comply.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Minimum 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, a number,
  and a special character.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Reuse of the last 5 passwords is not permitted.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote
  access to company systems, effective immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PHISHING AWARENESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Employees are reminded to:
- Never click links or open attachments from unknown senders.
- Verify unexpected requests for sensitive information by phone,
  using a known internal extension (not a number provided in the
  suspicious email).
- Report suspicious emails to security@acmecorp-example.com using
  the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DEVICE AND DATA HANDLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Company laptops must have disk encryption enabled at all times.
- USB storage devices are restricted; use approved cloud storage
  for file transfers.
- Lock your workstation (Windows+L / Cmd+Ctrl+Q) whenever stepping
  away from your desk.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. INCIDENT REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Any suspected security incident (lost device, compromised account,
malware infection) must be reported to the IT Helpdesk within
1 hour of discovery.

  IT Helpdesk: helpdesk@acmecorp-example.com
  Emergency Security Line: (555) 010-1234 (fictitious number)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------
All employees must acknowledge receipt of this policy via the HR
portal by January 31, 2026.

Questions may be directed to the IT Security Department at
itsecurity@acmecorp-example.com.

--- END OF MEMO ---
